# CampusConnect: Ask. Share. Grow Together.

> 🚧 **Project status: Under construction.** This repository is actively being built and will be updated soon. Stay tuned!

A peer-help and resource-sharing platform for college students, built for **DEXATHON 2026** (Open Innovation track, Software category) at the Department of Computer Applications, Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology.

## The Problem

Students rely on scattered WhatsApp groups, notice boards, and word of mouth to find help. Requests get buried, skilled peers never meet the people who need them, and useful items like textbooks, calculators, and lab kits sit unused while others buy new ones. First-year and shy students are affected the most.

## Our Solution

CampusConnect is a web app where students post what they need or can offer and get matched with peers on campus.

**Post → Match → Connect**

## Planned Features

- **Request and Offer Board:** post needs or offers by category (academic help, lend/borrow, project teammates, events)
- **Skill Profiles:** list the subjects you are strong in and the items you can lend
- **Smart Matching:** tag-based suggestions of the right peers
- **Safe Contact:** contact details are revealed only after both people accept
- **Karma Points and Badges:** rewards for helping others (e.g. "Top Mentor")

## Tech Stack

| Part | Technology |
